# Asset provenance

## Supplied portraits and work images

Chuka supplied the source images directly for this website on 10 August 2026 and confirmed that there are no licence restrictions on their use. Page-display derivatives are local WebP files. Press downloads preserve high-quality JPG derivatives. The site does not use generative edits of Chuka's likeness.
