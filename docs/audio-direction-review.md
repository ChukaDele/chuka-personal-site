# Ambient direction review

## Brief

The sound must suggest a contemporary Renaissance atelier: intellectual, cinematic, restrained, curious and slightly mysterious. It must not read as fantasy, tavern music, a corporate ident or a music video.

## Directions considered

### A. Print room after hours

Filtered room air, soft paper grain, low timber resonance and rare mechanical detail. The palette has material texture without a melody. It supports reading because it supplies place and tactility rather than demanding musical attention.

Risk: untreated noise can resemble HVAC. The selected render uses two filtered noise colours and a nearly subliminal 73 Hz foundation, with slow fades and section-specific filtering.

### B. Glass harmonic study

Sparse bowed-glass partials and unresolved intervals. This is cinematic and curious, but its tonal foreground can become spa-like, fantasy-coded or emotionally prescriptive during long reading passages.

Decision: rejected for the persistent bed. Its harmonic clarity would compete with the editorial voice.

### C. Clockwork drafting table

Measured ticks, wooden mechanisms and a restrained pulse. It makes construction and systems thinking legible, but repetition creates urgency and can turn the portfolio into a trailer.

Decision: rejected as ambience. Mechanical character is reserved for short threshold and handling cues.

## Selection

Direction A, **Print room after hours**, is the strongest fit. It keeps the atelier material, modern and credible. The cue palette borrows only the useful tactile qualities from Direction C. Direction B is excluded.

Evidence used in the selection:

- no melody or beat competes with long-form reading;
- broadband texture changes convincingly through filters, so sections can have distinct energy without loading separate tracks;
- local procedural generation provides an unambiguous commercial-use provenance chain;
- the 48-second asset compresses to roughly 590 KB and avoids runtime streaming;
- the final bus graph leaves substantial headroom and makes mute, duck and visibility changes immediate.

This is the implementation selection. The final remote experience review should still audition it on laptop speakers and headphones before production approval.
