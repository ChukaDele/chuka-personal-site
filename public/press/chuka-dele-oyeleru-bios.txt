LONG BIO
Chuka Dele-Oyeleru works in Strategy & Operations, focusing on problems that cross functional boundaries and arrive without a complete route to execution. At ETAP, he progressed from executive-support and operating work into broader operations leadership. He helped build the insurtech's first enterprise channel from market mapping and pricing through launch to seven-figure gross premiums, redesigned operational handoffs that reduced claims turnaround by 57%, and supported fundraising readiness and market expansion. At Rvysion, a 32-person cross-functional studio, his work connects commercial development, resource allocation and venture building; this has included 35% client-base growth and a 12% reduction in operating expenses. He is also building the operating model for The Bredge, a senior embedded data partner for growing businesses. Chuka trained in Quantity Surveying at the University of Lagos and is completing a full-time MBA at Alliance Manchester Business School. His speaking explores how teams move from ambition to operating reality, work across disciplines without losing ownership, and use AI as leverage while keeping consequential judgement human-owned.
