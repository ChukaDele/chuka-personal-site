# Project guidance

- Keep the primary professional classification as Strategy & Operations.
- Do not invent outcomes, case studies, resource endorsements or personal biography.
- Reuse the documented public-domain artwork. Add every new historical source to `content/artworks.ts`.
- Motion must reinforce observation becoming structure. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep unfinished route shells minimal and `noindex` until their content is verified.
- For SEO research, search-intent briefs, personal-brand content and LinkedIn search distribution, load the canonical Major `seo-os` skill and its `references/founder-led-search-experiment.md` playbook. Use the tested `scripts/seo-content-experiment.mjs` in `~/Projects/project-baracks` for evidence-gated preparation and live checks; see `docs/seo-publishing-workflow.md`.
- The owner-approved site owns the original indexable asset. A distinct LinkedIn article may reference it only after editorial and client-clearance approval. No paid/forced indexing, copied SERP pages or unreviewed publication.
- Website closeout requires successful GitHub `validate`, `site-quality` and `secret-scan` checks, an eligible human review under the active ruleset, and separately evidenced browser QA on an approved HTTPS preview. Static quality checks are not browser QA. Keep blocked stages explicit, never mark them complete or bypass branch protections.
- Do not commit credentials, local environment files, downloaded source generations or Cloudflare configuration secrets.
